About Us

Software Development Cell (SDC) is initiated to harness the technical skills of students. The SDC looks at development of projects that provides an opportunity for the students to work in an organized team structure and defined timelines. Students have the advantage of playing different roles such as software engineer, programmer, team-lead, management and maintenance. This helps them to get exposure to the complete life cycle of software development.
